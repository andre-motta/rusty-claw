# Waypoints for the fourteenth level at the original speeds, from the
# level's geometry. The route is searched a stretch at a time: each searched
# stretch below runs with its waypoints as --hints (with --hints-on-foot)
# and ends at its goal (--goal X,Y with --goal-on-foot), and the next
# carries on from it (--from-route, --from-tick at its last tick). Every
# stretch uses --seed level and --fragile-cost 100 unless it says
# otherwise.
#
# The route takes the long way through the temple, checkpoint to
# checkpoint, to the warp down to Omar's chamber. Several crossings need a
# timing the search's coarse graph does not see: an elevator met and left
# at the right moment, a rope swung from one, a jump in place on a toggle
# peg that waits for the pegs' cycle, and three ledges no jump reaches,
# climbed with a dynamite boost: a stick set off at Claw's side in mid-air
# (right or left and dynamite, after a jump) by the time a help screen of
# three seconds is up (the route's `@N menu help 3000` lines), then a jump
# from the blast. Those are written by hand: each one was found by playing
# the game from the stretch's start with every wait in a range and keeping
# a wait in the middle of three or more that all work. Held buttons are
# given as masks of the route file, in ticks counted from the stretch's
# first tick, each range inclusive.
