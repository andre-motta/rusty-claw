# Waypoints for the ninth level at the original speeds, from the level's
# geometry. The route is searched a stretch at a time: each searched
# stretch below runs with its waypoints as --hints (with --hints-on-foot)
# and ends at its goal (--goal X,Y), and the next carries on from it
# (--from-route, --from-tick at its last tick). Every stretch uses --seed
# level and --fragile-cost 100.
#
# The route takes the warp high above the start, which puts Claw at the
# foot of the shaft below the end of the level, and climbs the shaft in
# mid-air: no jump of his own reaches its first floor (the bridge with the
# two powder kegs). Three times he throws a stick of dynamite near the top
# of a jump and the help screen is up for three seconds at the boundary
# after the throw (the route's `@N menu help 3000` lines): the screen's
# time burns the fuse, so the stick goes off at once beside him, its blast
# stops him in mid-air, he stands there for a tick and a jump pressed then
# is a fresh jump from that height. A new game carries three sticks, one
# short of the bridge, so the fourth blast is a powder keg's: from below
# the bridge, at the top of a jump, Claw shoots the keg at its left end
# with the pistol, its blast stops him in mid-air too, and the jump from
# there lands him on the bridge. Each of those jumps reaches its height
# only with Catnip, which PegLeg on the deck by the first saw drops: the
# stretches from taking it to the keg's blast fit in its fifteen seconds.
#
