# and ends at its goal (--goal X,Y with --goal-on-foot), and the next
# carries on from it (--from-route, --from-tick at its last tick). Every
# stretch uses --seed level and --fragile-cost 100.

# The route takes the long way over the island, checkpoint to checkpoint,
# passing by both warps down to the caves under the island, up the shaft
# of toggle pegs to the boss room's door, and sits through RedTail's

# does not see: a rope whose swing must be met and left at the right
# moment, a row or a column of toggle pegs that are out only for a short
# part of their cycle, each in turn, or a water rock whose throw must meet
# a peg as it slides out.
# Those are written by hand: each one was found by playing the game from
# the stretch's start with every wait in a range and keeping a wait in the
# middle of three or more that all work. Held buttons are given as masks of

# range inclusive.
